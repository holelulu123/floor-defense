# Kenney Mini Characters (12 animated people, 32 clips each)

- Author: Kenney, https://kenney.nl/assets/mini-characters-1
- License: CC0 1.0 (kenney.nl/support; see LICENSE.txt). No attribution required.
- Files: glTF conversion copied 2026-10-30 from github.com/shorepine/kenney (`3d/mini-characters/`), which is the
  same conversion the Blocky Characters and Graveyard Kit copies came from. kenney.nl itself refuses non-browser
  downloads, so the zip was not fetched from there.
- Only the twelve characters (character-male-a..f, character-female-a..f) and `Textures/colormap.png` (referenced by
  the glb files by relative uri) were copied; mobility aids and wheelchairs were left out.
- Skinned rigs; clips: static, idle, walk, sprint, jump, fall, crouch, sit, drive, die, pick-up, emote-yes, emote-no,
  holding-*, attack-melee-left/right, attack-kick-left/right, interact-*, wheelchair-*.
- Used as the human bandits/pirates in Bandit Village and Pirate Cove (replacing the Kenney Blocky Characters there).
